🌍 The CIA Triad: Foundation of Cybersecurity



Confidentiality: Keeping sensitive information secure is the backbone of cybersecurity. Encryption, access control, and authentication help ensure that data is only accessible to authorized users.



Integrity: Data must remain accurate and unaltered. Hashing, digital signatures, and checksums help maintain integrity, preventing tampering or corruption.



Availability: Even the most secure data is useless if it’s not accessible. Redundancy, backups, and protection against DoS attacks help maintain availability, even during failures.
